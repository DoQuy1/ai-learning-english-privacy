Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: SpeakFlow AI keeps your learning data on your phone. We do not run accounts, we do not collect your name, email or contacts, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do so. Voice input uses your device's speech recognition service.

1. Who we are

SpeakFlow AI (“the app”, “we”) is an English-learning app published by Đỗ Ngọc Quý, an individual developer. Contact: doquy07072002@gmail.com.

2. Who the app is for

SpeakFlow AI is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us or uninstall the app, which removes the data stored on the device.

If you are under 18, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian where local law requires it (in some countries the age of digital consent is higher than 13, up to 16). Google states that it limits ad personalisation for users it knows to be under 18; see section 6.

3. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your choices: interface language, English level, learning goals, daily goal.
  • Learning progress: completed and in-progress lessons, answers, study time, streak, saved words and flashcard review state.
  • Temporary lesson unlocks earned by watching a rewarded ad, and counters used to limit how often full-screen ads appear.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Microphone, speech recognition and text-to-speech

  • The app asks for the microphone permission only to let you practise speaking with the AI Tutor. It listens only after you tap the microphone button. You can refuse the permission and type instead.
  • Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service of your device (usually provided by Google). That service may send audio to its provider according to the provider's own privacy policy. The app does not record, store or receive your audio.
  • Spoken English examples are produced by your device's text-to-speech engine.
  • In the current version the AI Tutor answers with pre-written demo replies and your messages are not sent to any server of ours. If a future version connects the tutor to an online service, we will update this policy and the Google Play data declarations before enabling it.

5. Accounts, purchases and analytics

The app has no sign-up or account system, and no in-app purchases are currently available. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, native, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID, IP address and approximate (IP-based) location;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and diagnostics;
  • information about ads you view or interact with.

Rules that apply:

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app shows Google's consent message before requesting ads. You can review your choice later in Profile → Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices when that option is available.
  • Teens. Google says it disables ad personalisation and restricts some ad categories for Google Accounts of users under 18. The app does not collect your age.
  • Control. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Ads are labelled “Ad” and are never shown during a speaking conversation or while you answer an exercise.

Learn more: How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we have no data about you to share. Data is handled by the third-party services described above (Google AdMob/Google Play services and your device's speech recognition provider) under their own policies.

8. Retention and your rights

Because the data is stored on your device, you control it and can delete it as described in section 3. Depending on where you live you may have rights over personal data held by Google or by other providers; please exercise them with that provider. For anything relating to the app, contact us at the address above.

9. Security

Data stays in the app's private storage on your device, and network connections made by the app use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

If we change how the app handles data (for example by adding accounts, an online AI tutor or purchases), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change takes effect.
