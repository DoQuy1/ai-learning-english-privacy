Your privacy matters.

Learn English with confidence. Understand what stays on your device, what is sent to our services, and how third parties are involved.

In short

SpeakFlow AI keeps your learning data on your phone. An account is optional: if you create one, your email address and learning progress are stored with our backend provider, Supabase. The AI Tutor and the “Explain with AI” button send the text of your question to our server and then to Google’s Gemini service to write the answer. We do not collect your contacts and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do so. Voice input uses your device’s speech recognition service.

Quick summary

4 core ideas

Local learning data

Your level, goals, study time, streak, saved words and flashcards stay on your phone. As a guest, nothing is sent to us.

Optional account

Sign in with email or Google to back up your progress. Learning works fully without an account.

Transparent advertising

Banner, native, interstitial and rewarded ads come from Google AdMob. Premium members see no ads.

Speech and AI

Your device turns speech into text. Text sent to the AI Tutor goes to our server and Google Gemini, and we do not store it.

Who the app is for

SpeakFlow AI is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us or uninstall the app, which removes the data stored on the device.

If you are under 18, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian where local law requires it (in some countries the age of digital consent is higher than 13, up to 16). Google states that it limits ad personalisation for users it knows to be under 18; see section 6.
3

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your choices: interface language, English level, learning goals, daily goal.
  • Learning progress: completed and in-progress lessons, answers, study time, streak, saved words, flashcard review state and the dictionary words you opened.
  • Temporary lesson unlocks earned by watching a rewarded ad, counters used to limit how often full-screen ads and our prompts appear, and the cached state of your subscription.
You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. If you use the app as a guest (without an account), this is the only copy and nothing is sent to us.
4

Microphone, speech recognition and the AI Tutor

The app asks for the microphone permission only to let you practise speaking with the AI Tutor. It listens only after you tap the microphone button. You can refuse the permission and type instead.
Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service of your device (usually provided by Google). That service may send audio to its provider according to the provider’s own privacy policy. The app does not record, store or receive your audio.
Spoken English examples and the tutor’s replies are read aloud by your device’s text-to-speech engine.
AI Tutor and “Explain with AI”. You need to be signed in. When you send a message (typed, or spoken and converted to text on your device) or ask for an explanation of a dictionary word, the app sends over HTTPS to our server (a Supabase Edge Function): the text, the recent messages of that conversation, the practice mode and level, your app language and, for a dictionary question, the word and its dictionary entry. Our server forwards this to Google’s Gemini API, which writes the answer. We do not store your messages or the answers. Our database keeps only technical usage data needed to enforce the daily time limit: when a session started and ended and the seconds counted.
Please do not type personal or sensitive information into the AI Tutor. We use the free tier of the Gemini API. According to Google’s terms for such services, Google may use submitted content to provide and improve its products, and people may review it. See Gemini API terms.
5

Accounts, purchases and analytics

Optional account. You can learn without an account. If you choose to create one, you can sign up with an email address and password, or continue with Google. The app sends the following to Supabase (our database and authentication provider) over HTTPS:

  • your email address and a password, which Supabase Auth stores only as a salted hash (we never see or store your password). If you continue with Google, Google gives Supabase your email address and name; we never receive your Google password;
  • a random user ID;
  • your learning progress: which lessons you started or completed, number of completions, best score, the first-try result of each exercise, and study seconds per day;
  • optionally a display name and your preferred language.

We use this only to let you sign in, restore your progress on a new phone and keep it backed up. Access rules in the database allow each signed-in user to read and change only their own rows. We do not store your voice recordings or AI conversations, and we do not store whether you are a Premium subscriber in this database (that is managed by Google Play). Supabase also processes technical data needed to run the service (for example IP addresses in logs) under its own policy. Verification and password-reset emails are sent for you by Supabase.

Deleting your account. In the app: Profile, Account, Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your progress stored with Supabase; progress on your phone stays until you clear it. You can also ask us by email (section 11). If you only sign out, the cloud copy remains.

Feedback. If you are signed in you can send feedback from the app (Profile, Send feedback), once per week. We receive the text you type (category, subject, message and an optional reply email), your account ID, the time and the app version. You cannot attach files, photos or recordings. It is stored with Supabase and forwarded by email to the developer.

Reminders and sounds. Study reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device using the notification permission; no reminder data is sent to us. Sound effects and vibration are played locally.

Premium subscription. The app offers an optional yearly subscription, billed through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google: we never see your card or payment details. To unlock Premium the app receives purchase information from Google Play (such as the product, a purchase token and the purchase state) and keeps a small record on your device saying that Premium was active and when it was last confirmed. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

Rating. The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play’s in-app review. Whether and how that dialog appears is controlled by Google, and we do not receive your rating through the app.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.
6

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, native, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID, IP address and approximate (IP-based) location;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and diagnostics;
  • information about ads you view or interact with.

Rules that apply

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app shows Google’s consent message before requesting ads. You can review your choice later in Profile → Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices when that option is available.

Teens

Google says it disables ad personalisation and restricts some ad categories for Google Accounts of users under 18. The app does not collect your age.

Control

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Labels and experience

Ads are labelled “Ad”, are never shown during a speaking conversation or while you answer an exercise, and are not shown to Premium subscribers. Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is handled by the third-party services described above, under their own policies: Supabase (only if you create an account), Google Gemini (only for AI Tutor and “Explain with AI” messages), Google AdMob and Google Play services, and your device’s speech recognition provider.

Retention and your rights

Data on your device is under your control and can be deleted as described in section 3. Account data is kept until you delete your account (or ask us to). We do not keep your AI messages or the answers. Depending on where you live you may have rights over personal data held by Google or by other providers; please exercise them with that provider. For anything relating to the app, contact us at the address in section 11.

Security

Data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, and network connections made by the app use HTTPS. Account data is protected by Supabase Auth and database access rules (Row Level Security). No method of storage is perfectly secure.
